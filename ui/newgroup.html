New launch group

What kind of group?

Every launch group gets a client profile folder of its own, built from a copy of your settings, so the profiles you already have stay exactly as they are. You go through it one step at a time.

Groups made here

Name

What the group is called here, in Your accounts and in the EVE launcher.

Who's in it

For each account pick one character, or Login screen to have it start at the login screen with no character (Character “None” in the launcher). Click again to take it out.

Settings to start from

Keys, shortcuts and the sound & graphics the next step changes are copied from this character's profile.

Window

One window for every client of the group: they all open in the same spot and size (they share this profile). Peepshow's previews show which client is which.

“Show it on screen” puts a stand-in EVE window there: drag it where the clients should open (its edges stick to the screen's sides and middle). The numbers follow it.

Pedal / hotkeys

Keys that bring the group's next or previous client to the front (optional).

    Editing keeps everything the clients saved in this group's folder (windows, overview, modules). Members you add get settings as step 4 says; characters no member needs leave the folder.
