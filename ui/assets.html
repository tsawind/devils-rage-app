    Coffins: characters sitting in a freighter, Bowhead or capital. An Alpha can still log in inside a hull it can't fly (Omega-only hulls), use its hangars and log out again, but only one Alpha can be logged in at a time across all accounts, it can't undock it, and if it leaves the ship it can't board again. A weekend Omega lets several log in together.

    Wormhole space has no asset safety: when a structure dies, part of its fit, the core and a wreck drop, and everyone's hangars spill into containers the attackers can loot or shoot (show info shows whose they are). Every clone in it dies. Repackaging a ship destroys its rigs.
