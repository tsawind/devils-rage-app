Client tuning

Guide: set up a group of clients start to finish
  1. Make the launch group. Your accounts: type a name (e.g. “Test Launch”) in the new-group box, Add, then tick it on a few characters on different accounts (the launcher starts one character per account).
  2. See it in the app. Launch groups → ↻ Refresh. Give it pedal keys (Next / Prev).
  3. Build the baseline. Start one of them through the EVE launcher, arrange windows, overview and shortcuts the way you want everyone, then log out and close that client (EVE saves its settings on close).
  4. Copy it to the group. With every client closed: Client settings → Set up a launch group → pick the group and the baseline → Copy to the group.
  5. Tune sound and graphics. Here: pick a preset below (Alt for the group, Main for your own client), pick the group's profiles, Apply. Music off, alarms and wormhole activations kept.
  6. Match the EVE launcher once. On the group's card in Launch groups, open “Set this group up in the EVE launcher” and follow it (same accounts, client profile numbers as shown).
  7. Launch. ▶ Open launcher, launch the group, then Multibox previews and cycle with the pedal.
