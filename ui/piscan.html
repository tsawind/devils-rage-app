BETA
1

Client and planet

In EVE: open the planet, turn on the scan for a resource. This page only watches the picture; it never clicks or types in EVE.
2

The planet's edge

The red circle should sit on the planet's edge. Find edge guesses; Click 3 on the edge is exact (click three points spread round the rim). Drag the middle to move it, mouse wheel to size it. Zooming in EVE is fine afterwards: the circle follows (keep some of the rim in view).
3

Point out 3 of your buildings

Freeze, pick a kind of building below, click one of those on the picture (a magnifier helps). Look-alikes (two storage facilities) don't matter: the app works out which is which. Three or more spread out (extractor heads are good) tells which way the planet faces; afterwards green rings sit on every building.
4

Scan

In EVE first: in the planet's Scan panel drag both ends of the colour band right out to the ends (the widest band) and leave it there, so every scan uses the same scale. Then pick the resource EVE is showing here, press Scan and turn the planet slowly (about a quarter turn every few seconds) until the bar is full. Switch the resource in EVE and here for the next one without moving. Drifting? Pick a kind of building and click one.
5

Save for the corp

Adds to what the corp already has for this planet and resource (the Corp map tab on the PI screen shows it, with the best places to put two extractors).
