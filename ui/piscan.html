BETA
1

Client and planet

In EVE: open the planet, turn on the scan for a resource. This page only watches the picture; it never clicks or types in EVE.
2

The planet's edge

The red circle should sit on the planet's edge. Find edge does it; or drag the circle's middle, and the mouse wheel over the picture resizes it. Don't zoom in EVE after this.
3

Point out 3 of your buildings

Freeze, pick a building below, click it on the picture. Three spread out (extractor heads help) tells which way the planet faces. Afterwards the green rings should sit on every building.
4

Scan

Pick the resource EVE is showing, press Scan, then turn the planet slowly (about a quarter turn every few seconds) until the bar is full. Switch the resource in EVE and here to scan the next one without moving. Drifting? Pick a building and click it on the picture.
5

Save for the corp

Adds to what the corp already has for this planet and resource (the Corp map tab on the PI screen shows it, with the best places to put two extractors).
