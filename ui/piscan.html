BETA
1

Client and planet

In EVE: open the planet, turn on the scan for a resource. This page only watches the picture; it never clicks or types in EVE.
2

The planet's edge

EVE always puts the planet in the middle of the window, so only its size is needed: Click the edge, then click once anywhere on the planet's edge. Mouse wheel over the picture fine-tunes it. Zoomed in EVE after locking on? Click the edge again: it keeps the lock and works out the new distance.
3

Lock on

Or point out buildings yourself
Not your colony? Anyone's buildings work: in EVE right-click the planet → Show other characters' networks. Freeze, pick a kind of building below, click one of those on the picture (a magnifier helps). Look-alikes (two storage facilities) don't matter: the app works out which is which. Three or more spread out (extractor heads are good) tells which way the planet faces; afterwards green rings sit on every building.
4

Scan

Once per planet: pick Bare planet below with EVE's scan on No filter, press Scan and turn the planet all the way round. The app learns the planet's own colours (lava, terrain) and takes them out of every resource scan. Then, in EVE: in the planet's Scan panel drag both ends of the colour band right out to the ends (the widest band) and leave it there, so every scan uses the same scale. Then pick the resource EVE is showing here, press Scan and turn the planet slowly (about a quarter turn every few seconds) until the bar is full. Switch the resource in EVE and here for the next one without moving. Drifting? Pick a kind of building and click one.
5

Save for the corp

Adds to what the corp already has for this planet and resource (the Corp map tab on the PI screen shows it, with the best places to put two extractors).
