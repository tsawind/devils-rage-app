DEVIL'S RAGE APP: WHAT IT DOES WITH YOUR DATA

The app is a window around our corp's own sites: the mapper (devilsrage.duckdns.org) and SeAT (seatdevilsrage.duckdns.org). It has no accounts of its own, holds no EVE logins or passwords, and sends nothing to anyone else.

Reads on your PC:
- EVE's Local chat logs, only to see which system each of your characters is in.
- The clipboard, only to notice a probe scan you copied in EVE. Anything else you copy is ignored.
- The title of the window in front ("EVE - <name>"), only to know whose probe scan was copied.
It never reads EVE's memory and never sends keys or clicks to EVE.

Sends:
- Your characters' current system, to our mapper (turn off: Settings > Location).
- Probe scans you copy in EVE, to our mapper (turn off: Settings > Probe scans).
- A request for app settings to our mapper, with nothing about you in it.
- An update check to GitHub. Installing an update always asks you first.

Full privacy note: https://github.com/tsawind/devils-rage-app/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
Uninstall any time from Windows Settings > Apps.
